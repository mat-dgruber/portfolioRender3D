# Design State: 3D Render Studio Landing Page

_Last updated: 2026-05-10 by design-builder_

## Brief
- **Problem:** Architects struggle to communicate emotional impact via 2D plants; needs high-end visual conversion.
- **Primary persona:** Busy architects seeking premium technical partners.
- **Success metric:** Lighthouse ≥ 90; "High End" feeling in < 3s; high WhatsApp conversion.
- **Brief document:** docs/designpowers/briefs/2026-05-10-render-studio.md

## Personas
- **The Busy Architect** — Needs quick visual validation of quality and reliability.
- **The Marketing Developer** — Needs a brand-aligned visual engine for property launches.

## Design Principles
1. **The Art of the Silence** — Negative space is as important as the renders. Use generous padding.
2. **Narrative Clarity** — Accessibility is the foundation. Semantic headings (H1-H3) drive the story.
3. **Frictionless Intent** — Conversion (WhatsApp FAB) is always present but aesthetically integrated.
4. **Zero-Friction Transformation** — Interactivity (Before/After slider) must be effortless and preloaded.

## Taste Profile
- **Emotional target:** "Sleek confidence", "Exclusive precision"
- **Quality level:** Flagship
- **Key references:** High-end architectural photography, Art Gallery minimal aesthetics.
- **Aesthetic principles:** Negative space, Dark/Gold contrast, Type-driven hierarchy.
